Lash Care

Keep them full between visits.

1First 24 hours

Keep them dry

Skip steam, saunas and long hot showers while the adhesive cures.

2Every morning

Brush through

A gentle spoolie brush keeps every fan fluffy and in place.

3A few times a week

Lash bath

Oil-free foam cleanser, rinse, pat dry. Clean lashes last longer.

4Every 2–3 weeks

Book your fill

Natural lashes shed daily. A fill brings your set back to full.
