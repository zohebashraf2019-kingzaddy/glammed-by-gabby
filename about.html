My story

Glam is a feeling.

Glammed By Gabby started with one simple goal: create a safe, welcoming place where everyone can relax, get pampered and walk out confident. Many of my clients have been with me for years — some since day one — and that trust means everything to me.

Every set and every brow is customized. I look at your natural lashes, eye shape and skin before anything starts, so the result looks like you, just more glammed. Healthy natural lashes always come first — no fallout, no irritation.

When I’m not in the studio on Broadway Blvd, you’ll find me between Reno and Hollywood, LA, keeping up with the latest lash and brow trends to bring back to my chair.
